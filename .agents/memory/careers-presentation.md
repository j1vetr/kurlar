---
name: Careers presentation preferences
description: User direction for the careers page palette and labeling
---

The careers page should use white tones, not cream or dark gray backgrounds. Do not use brown accents or A, B, C, D labels for benefits.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected those choices in the first redesign.

**How to apply:** Keep future careers page styling in white tones with the existing brand blue for accents. Write professional, role-specific requirements without em dashes or semicolons.
