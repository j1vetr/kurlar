---
name: Permanent catalogue QR destination
description: Catalogue page must remain stable across yearly editions because it will be used on printed QR codes
---

Keep `/katalog` as the permanent public catalogue address when yearly PDF editions change. The user wants the PDF to open immediately in a viewport-filling reader, with download and new-tab controls on the right. Do not add a cover or marketing section before the reader.

**Why:** The user needs a direct catalogue QR code for kurlar.com.tr. Printed QR codes must not require replacement when the catalogue is updated.

**How to apply:** Replace edition-specific PDF assets and year labels while preserving the page URL. Do not switch the QR destination to a dated PDF filename.
